# thesis.fin

- This study evaluates the CANSLIM investment strategy in Vietnam’s frontier market using machine learning techniques. Based on a dataset of 1,582 stocks during 2024, it implements an automated screening process to assess whether structured investment signals related to growth, investor demand, and market trends can effectively predict stock and portfolio performance. The study applied a three-month backtesting design and conducted two predictive tasks: return regression at the stock level and market outperformance classification at the portfolio level. Results show that ensemble learning models, including Random Forest and Voting techniques, delivered significantly higher predictive accuracy compared to traditional linear approaches and highlight a distinction in predictive factors: short-term growth signals dominate at the stock level, while market conditions guide portfolio-level outcomes. CANSLIM-based portfolios also yield higher returns and risk-adjusted performance than traditional assets such as gold and foreign exchange. These findings support the integration of rule-based logic with machine learning in emerging markets, offering improved performance, transparency, and practical insights for investors, fund managers, and policymakers seeking to improve investment strategy and market efficiency.
- Based on the idea of creating a product to recommend stock codes using multiple filters combined with machine learning, the product identifies stocks that outperform the market. It enables real-time recommendations and analysis.
